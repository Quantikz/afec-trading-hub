The company

About AFEC

A Nigerian private company limited by shares, incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020.

Who we are

AFEC Trading Hub & Business Resources Ltd was incorporated on 12 February 2025. The registered office is in Nigeria, with operations recorded at Mustapha Katagum Plaza, Gidan Gona Road, Bauchi, Bauchi State.

The company was formed to combine education — including Islamic and academic programmes — with practical enterprise: a trading and entrepreneurship academy, vocational training, placements, and general commercial activity.

Our public academy work is summed up in a simple line: building a generation of freedom.

What we do

Education, enterprise and trade under one roof.

Our objects, as registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission, cover academic services, Islamic education, human-capital development and a full range of commercial activities.

01

Academic & Islamic Education

Nursery to secondary pathways, NCE and diploma programmes, Islamic moral lessons, Qur’anic memorisation, libraries and bookshops.

02

Trading Academy

A dedicated trading and entrepreneurship academy — market skill, discipline and community for people ready to turn knowledge into capital.

03

Vocational & HR Training

Workshops, seminars, skill acquisition for youth and women, and consultancy in Islamic and Arabic studies for public and private clients.

04

Placements & Admissions

Educational placement services and processing of foreign / overseas admission for students.

05

Trade & Merchandise

General contracts, distribution, electricals and electronics, manufacturers’ representation, imports and exports.

06

Logistics & Allied Services

Road transport and haulage, real estate services, agro-allied activity, catering and related commercial services.
